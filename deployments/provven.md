**dApp name:** Provven (portable Driver Qualification / career-card proofs)

**Contract repository:** https://github.com/blueOctorok/resume-wallet — Compact circuits in [`compact/`](https://github.com/blueOctorok/resume-wallet/tree/main/compact) (`cdl-class`, `cdl-endorsements`, `cdl-restrictions`, `med-cert-valid`, plus `mvr-clean-36`, `cdl-class-a`, `previous-employer-verified`).

**Brief description:** Provven is a **regulated-industry** hiring product (trucking DQ files). Candidates never touch a wallet. A **server-managed** Midnight wallet submits Compact proofs that a third-party MVR fact holds (CDL class, endorsements, restrictions, medical-cert validity, clean-MVR window). Public outputs are the disclosed predicate + hashes. Raw report bytes, name, SSN, and DL stay off-chain. We are **already live on Preprod** (same circuits, hosted proof server, in-app “Proven on Midnight · derived from Accio”). Mainnet is the same path, not a new architecture.

Circuits are **billboards**, not vaults: `assert` the witness matches the public disclosure, insert a pull **nullifier** (replay guard), overwrite a single `factCommitment`. No user funds, no escrow, no token.
